# Code of conduct

Courmap is a small open-source project run by volunteers. We want it to be a pleasant place to fix
anime episode numbering together.

- Be kind and assume good faith. Disagree about the work, not the person.
- No harassment, personal attacks, or discrimination of any kind.
- Keep issues and pull requests on topic, and give contributors time to respond.
- Do not publish other people's private information.

Maintainers may edit, close or remove contributions, and block people who break these rules. To report
a problem, use [private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/courmap/courmap/security/advisories/new)
and say it is a conduct report, or contact a maintainer through a private channel listed on their GitHub profile.

This policy follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
